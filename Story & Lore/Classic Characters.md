Characters are important figures in the story of **The Classic: The Return of Korblox**.

They represent different parts of Roblox history and help guide players through the event.

---

# Classic Characters

Classic characters are inspired by Roblox history.

They help guide players through the event and represent different parts of the classic era.

---

## Classic Builder

A creator who represents the builders who shaped Roblox.

Purpose:

- Guides players
- Explains the importance of classic creations
- Helps restore damaged worlds

## Classic Guest

A mysterious character connected to the guest era.

Purpose:

- Introduces players to the classic timeline
- Provides story quests
- Helps players understand the Korblox invasion

[Classic Guest](Characters/Classic%20Guest.md)

## Classic Noob

A symbol of classic Roblox.

Purpose:

- Represents new players
- Shows that anyone can become part of Roblox history

Design:

- Classic avatar colors
- Simple appearance
- Recognizable style

Note:

Players can already recreate the classic Noob appearance using Roblox body colors.

Related:

[Classic Noob](Characters/Classic%20Noob.md)
