The timeline of the Korblox invasion.

---

## Before The Invasion

The Korblox discover the power hidden within classic Roblox history.

---

## The Discovery

The Korblox create a method of traveling through time.

---

## The Invasion

The Korblox arrive in 2008 and begin changing history.

---

## The Resistance

Players and classic characters fight back.

They recover:

- Lost [Tixs](../Gameplay%20Systems/Tixs%20System.md)
- Builder creations
- Classic locations

---

## The Final Battle

Players defeat the Korblox Leader and restore the timeline.
