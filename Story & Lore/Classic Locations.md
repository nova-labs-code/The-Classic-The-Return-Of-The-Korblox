Classic locations are areas inspired by different parts of Roblox history.

Each location should feel like a place from the past while supporting modern gameplay.

---

## Classic Roblox Plaza

The main starting area.

Features:

- Event hub
- Quest boards
- Reward shops
- Developer portals

[Event Hub](../Event/Event%20Hub)
