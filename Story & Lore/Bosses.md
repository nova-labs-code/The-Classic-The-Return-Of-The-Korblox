Bosses are powerful enemies that players fight during the event.

Each boss represents a different stage of the Korblox invasion.

---

# Korblox Warrior

## Role
The first major Korblox enemy players encounter.

## Location
A classic Roblox world taken over by the Korblox.

## Rewards
- Tixs
- Boss Badge

---

# Korblox General

## Role
A high-ranking Korblox commander.

The General controls important parts of the timeline and protects stolen classic items.

## Abilities
- Summons Korblox soldiers
- Creates obstacles
- Controls the battlefield

## Rewards
- Builder Tokens
- Rare Badge

---

# The Korblox Leader

## Role
The final boss of the event.

The Korblox Leader wants to permanently rewrite Roblox history.

## Battle

Players must work together to defeat the leader and restore the timeline.

## Rewards
- Exclusive Badge
- Special Builder Token reward
- Final event reward
