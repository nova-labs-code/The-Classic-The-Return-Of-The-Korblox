The Classic Guest represents the guest era of Roblox.

---

## Role

The Classic Guest is a mysterious character connected to Roblox history.

---

## Purpose

The Classic Guest introduces players to the classic timeline and helps explain what was lost.

---

## Story

During the Korblox invasion, the Classic Guest discovers that parts of the past are disappearing.

They help players recover forgotten parts of Roblox history.

---

## Quests

Possible quests:

- Find lost memories
- Explore classic locations
- Recover missing items
- Discover hidden secrets

---

## Personality

Traits:

- Curious
- Friendly
- Mysterious
- Adventurous

---

## Importance

The Classic Guest represents an important era of Roblox identity and community.
