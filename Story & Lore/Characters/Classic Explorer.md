The Classic Explorer is an adventurer who knows the hidden parts of the classic timeline.

---

## Role

The Classic Explorer guides players through forgotten locations and secrets.

---

## Purpose

The Classic Explorer helps players discover:

- Hidden areas
- Lost items
- Secret locations
- Classic worlds

---

## Story

After the Korblox changed history, many locations became hidden.

The Classic Explorer helps players find what was lost.

---

## Quests

Possible quests:

- Explore hidden areas
- Find collectibles
- Discover secrets
- Unlock forgotten locations

---

## Personality

Traits:

- Adventurous
- Smart
- Brave
- Curious

---

## Importance

The Classic Explorer represents the players who explored Roblox worlds.
