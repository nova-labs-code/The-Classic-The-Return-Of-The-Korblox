The Timeline Guardian protects Roblox history from being changed.

---

## Role

The Timeline Guardian watches over the different Roblox eras.

---

## Purpose

The Timeline Guardian helps players understand:

- Time travel
- Timeline changes
- The Korblox invasion

---

## Story

When the Korblox traveled back to 2008, the Timeline Guardian detected changes to history.

They call upon players to restore the timeline.

---

## Quests

Possible quests:

- Repair timeline damage
- Protect classic locations
- Recover lost artifacts
- Stop Korblox changes

---

## Personality

Traits:

- Wise
- Responsible
- Calm
- Protective

---

## Importance

The Timeline Guardian connects every part of the event story together.
