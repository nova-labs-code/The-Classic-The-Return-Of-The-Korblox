The Classic Noob represents the simple and recognizable Roblox avatar style.

---

## Role

The Classic Noob represents every player joining the adventure.

---

## Purpose

The Classic Noob shows that anyone can become part of Roblox history.

---

## Design

The Classic Noob uses:

- Classic body colors
- Simple appearance
- Recognizable Roblox style

Players can recreate this appearance themselves by changing their avatar body colors.

---

## Story

The Classic Noob joins the fight to protect the classic timeline from the Korblox invasion.

---

## Personality

Traits:

- Brave
- Friendly
- Curious
- Determined

---

## Importance

The Classic Noob represents the community of Roblox players.
