The Classic Builder represents the creators who shaped Roblox during the classic era.

---

## Role

The Classic Builder is a guide who helps players restore classic Roblox locations damaged by the Korblox invasion.

---

## Purpose

The Classic Builder teaches players about:

- Building
- Creativity
- Community creations
- Roblox history

---

## Story

When the Korblox attacked the timeline, many classic creations were damaged.

The Classic Builder asks players to help repair these worlds and recover lost creations.

---

## Quests

Possible quests:

- Repair classic structures
- Find missing building parts
- Restore old locations
- Help rebuild damaged areas

---

## Personality

Traits:

- Creative
- Helpful
- Inventive
- Determined

---

## Importance

The Classic Builder represents the builders who created the worlds players remember.
