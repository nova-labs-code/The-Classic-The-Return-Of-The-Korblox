## Overview

The Korblox are a powerful group from the future who discovered the importance of the classic Roblox era.

They believe the old Roblox timeline holds the greatest power ever created.

---

## Goal

The Korblox want to rewrite history by taking control of the classic era.

Their plan:
1. Travel back to 2008
2. Capture important locations
3. Steal the power of the old era
4. Create a new Roblox timeline

---

## The Korblox Army

The Korblox have different ranks:

## Korblox Soldier

The basic Korblox fighter.

Used to protect captured areas and stop players.

---

## Korblox Warrior

A stronger fighter equipped for battles.

Usually guards important locations.

---

## Korblox General

A commander who leads the invasion.

Controls major parts of the timeline.

---

## Korblox Leader

The strongest Korblox.

The final enemy standing between players and restoring Roblox history.
