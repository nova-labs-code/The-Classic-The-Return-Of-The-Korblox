The Classic Roblox Era represents the time period players travel back to during the event.

---

## Era Description

This era represents the early days of Roblox.

Important themes:

- Building
- Creativity
- Community
- Exploration

---

## Classic Features

The era includes:

- Blocky avatars
- Simple worlds
- Classic clothing
- Builder culture
- Old-style gameplay

---

## Importance

The Classic Era is the foundation that the Korblox are trying to control.

Players must protect it to preserve Roblox history.
