## Before The Event

For years, the classic era of Roblox has been remembered as one of the most important times in Roblox history.

The worlds created during this era became legends, and the items, games, and memories from that time were preserved.

Unknown to players, something was waiting beyond the timeline.

---

# 2018 - The Korblox Discovery

The Korblox discovered ancient records of the classic Roblox era.

They learned that the power of the old era came from:
- [Tixs](../Gameplay%20Systems/Tixs%20System)
- [Builder Tokens](../Gameplay%20Systems/Builder%20Tokens%20System)
- Classic creations made by the community

The Korblox believed that if they controlled the past, they could control the future.

Using a time machine, they traveled back to 2008.

---

# 2008 - The Classic Era

The Korblox arrive in the middle of classic Roblox.

The world is still simple:
- Blocky avatars
- Classic clothing
- Old-style games
- Builders creating new worlds

The Korblox begin changing history by taking control of important locations and stealing pieces of the classic era.

---

# The Player's Mission

Players are chosen to protect Roblox history.

They must:
- Explore classic worlds
- Complete [quests](../Gameplay%20Systems/Quest%20System)
- Recover lost [Tixs](../Gameplay%20Systems/Tixs%20System)
- Earn [Builder Tokens](<Builder%20Tokens%20System>)
- Defeat Korblox forces

---

# The Final Battle

The Korblox prepare their final attack to permanently change Roblox history.

Players must defeat their strongest leader and restore the timeline.

After the battle:
- Classic worlds are saved
- Tixs return
- Builders continue creating
- The legacy of Roblox lives on
