The Builder Token Shop contains exclusive UGC accessory rewards.

Players use [Builder Tokens](../Gameplay%20Systems/Builder%20Tokens%20System) to unlock special classic-themed accessories.

---

## Purpose

Builder Tokens represent harder achievements and reward players who complete challenging parts of the event.

They are earned through:

- Hard quests
- Boss battles
- Difficult challenges

---

## Reward Tiers

Builder Token rewards are organized into tiers.

---

## Tier 1

Entry-level UGC accessories.

Designed for players who complete some harder challenges.

Examples:

- Classic Toolbox
- Builder Helmet

---

## Tier 2

More exclusive UGC accessories.

Designed for players who complete multiple challenges.

Examples:

- Time Capsule Backpack
- Legacy Trophy

---

## Tier 3

The highest tier of UGC accessories.

Designed for the most dedicated players.

Examples:

- Tix Crown
- Korblox Relic
- Classic Champion Accessory

---

## Reward Requirements

Each accessory requires a specific amount of Builder Tokens to unlock.

Requirements should increase by tier.

---

## Game Reward Limits

Each participating game can provide:

- 1 Builder Token badge
- 1 Builder Token reward

This keeps every game unique and prevents players from farming one experience.

---

## Saving

The system should save:

- Builder Tokens
- Claimed accessories
- Reward ownership
