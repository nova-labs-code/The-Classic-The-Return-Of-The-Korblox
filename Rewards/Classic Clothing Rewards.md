Classic clothing is unlocked using [Tixs](../Gameplay%20Systems/Tixs%20System.md).

These rewards should feel like clothing from the classic Roblox era.

---

## Clothing Style

Classic clothing should use:

- Simple designs
- Bright colors
- Blocky Roblox style
- Easy-to-recognize themes

Avoid:
- Modern realistic clothing
- Overly detailed designs
- Trends that do not match classic Roblox
