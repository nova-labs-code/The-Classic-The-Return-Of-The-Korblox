Builder Token rewards are exclusive UGC items unlocked by earning [Builder Tokens](../Gameplay%20Systems/Builder%20Tokens%20System).

These rewards should feel like classic Roblox collectibles while still being desirable for modern players.

---

## UGC Design Rules

UGC should have:

- Classic Roblox inspiration
- Simple recognizable shapes
- Nostalgic themes
- Collectible value

Avoid:

- Overly modern designs
- Extremely detailed realistic items
- Designs that do not fit the classic theme

---

## Outfit Rewards

The main Builder Token rewards are full classic-themed outfits.

## Accessory Ideas

Possible additional rewards:

## Tix Crown

Theme:
A crown made from the lost Tix currency.

---

## Time Capsule Backpack

Theme:
A backpack containing memories from classic Roblox.

---

## Classic Builder Toolbox

Theme:
A creator accessory inspired by old building games.

---

## Legacy Trophy

Theme:
A reward showing that the player completed difficult challenges.

---

## Korblox Relic

Theme:
A collectible item connected to the Korblox invasion.

---

## Final Reward

A special reward for completing the entire event.

Possible ideas:

- Legacy Crown
- Roblox Time Capsule
- Golden Builder Token
- Classic Champion Outfit

---

## Reward Progression

Players progress through:

Easy Quests
→ Earn Tixs
→ Unlock Classic Clothing

Hard Quests
→ Earn Builder Tokens
→ Unlock UGC Rewards

Full Completion
→ Unlock Final Event Reward
