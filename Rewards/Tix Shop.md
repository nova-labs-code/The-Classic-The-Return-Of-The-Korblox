The Tix Shop allows players to spend [Tixs](../Gameplay%20Systems/Tixs%20System) on classic clothing and accessible event rewards.

---

## Purpose

The Tix Shop gives every player a way to earn classic rewards through normal gameplay.

Players should be able to participate without needing to complete the hardest challenges.

---

## Reward Categories

## Classic Clothing

Examples:

- Classic Builder Shirt
- Classic Explorer Outfit
- Retro Gamer Clothing
- Adventure Clothing

Related:

[Classic Clothing Rewards](Classic%20Clothing%20Rewards)

---

## Accessories

Possible items:

- Classic backpack
- Builder tools
- Retro accessories
- Event-themed items

---

## Pricing

Rewards should have different costs.

Example:

Common items:
- Low Tix cost

Rare clothing:
- Higher Tix cost

The goal is to reward players who spend more time in the event.

---

## Shop Features

The shop should include:

- Item preview
- Price display
- Purchase button
- Owned indicator
- Reward information

---

## Saving

The system should save:

- Purchased items
- Remaining Tixs
- Reward ownership
