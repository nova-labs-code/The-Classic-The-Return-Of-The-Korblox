Builder Tokens unlock full classic-themed UGC outfits.

These outfits should represent different types of classic Roblox players.

---

## Classic Builder

Theme:
The creators who built Roblox worlds.

Style:
- Builder clothing
- Classic work outfit
- Builder accessories

---

## Classic Guest

Theme:
The guest era of Roblox.

Style:
- Guest-inspired clothing
- Simple classic design
- Recognizable guest theme

---

## Classic Explorer

Theme:
Players who explored classic worlds.

Style:
- Adventure clothing
- Backpack
- Explorer accessories

---

## Classic Warrior

Theme:
Classic fighting games.

Style:
- Simple warrior clothing
- Classic battle accessories
- Retro fighter theme

---

## Classic Gamer

Theme:
Classic Roblox players.

Style:
- Retro clothing
- Gaming accessories
- Old-school player style

---

## Classic Veteran

Theme:
Players who remember the old Roblox era.

Style:
- Premium classic look
- Legacy accessories
- Special details

---

## Free Classic Look

Classic Noob is not a Builder Token reward.

Players can already recreate the classic Noob appearance using Roblox body colors.

The Builder Token outfits should provide unique designs players cannot easily recreate.
