# Reward Requirements

Reward requirements determine how much progress players need before unlocking event rewards.

The goal is to make rewards feel valuable while keeping progression fair.

---

# Tix Clothing Requirements

Tixs are used to unlock classic clothing rewards.

Examples:

## Classic Clothing

Requirement:

- Collect a certain amount of Tixs

Examples:

- Classic shirts
- Classic pants
- Full clothing sets

# Builder Token UGC Requirements

Builder Tokens are used to unlock UGC accessories.

Builder Token rewards use tiers.

---

## Tier 1

Requirement:

- Lower amount of Builder Tokens

Designed for players who complete some hard quests.

---

## Tier 2

Requirement:

- Medium amount of Builder Tokens

Designed for players who complete multiple challenges.

---

## Tier 3

Requirement:

- Highest amount of Builder Tokens

Designed for players who complete the most difficult challenges.

---

Tiers determine the progression needed to unlock the reward.

They do not represent item rarity.
