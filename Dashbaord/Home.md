# The Classic: The Return of Korblox

Welcome to the planning vault for **The Classic: The Return of Korblox**.

A Roblox community event where players travel back to the classic era of Roblox to stop the Korblox invasion, complete quests, collect [Tixs](../Gameplay%20Systems/Tixs%20System), earn [Builder Tokens](../Gameplay%20Systems/Builder%20Tokens%20System), and unlock classic rewards.

---

## Event Overview

The Korblox have discovered a way to travel through time. Their goal is to invade the classic Roblox era before its history can be preserved.

Players must travel through classic-inspired worlds, complete challenges, and restore Roblox history.
