Quest examples for The Classic: The Return of Korblox.

Quests should encourage exploration, teamwork, and progression.

---

## Easy Quest Examples

## Find The Lost Tix

Objective:

Find hidden Tixs around a classic world.

Reward:

- Tixs
- Easy quest badge

Related:

[Tixs System](Tixs%20System)

---

## Help The Builder

Objective:

Collect missing building parts and help repair a classic location.

Reward:

- Tixs
- Classic clothing progress

Related:

[Classic Builder](../Story%20&%20Lore/Characters/Classic%20Builder)

---

## Explore The Classic World

Objective:

Visit important locations from the classic era.

Reward:

- Tixs
- Explorer badge

Related:

[Classic Locations](../Story%20&%20Lore/Classic%20Locations)

# Hard Quest Examples

## Defeat The Korblox Warrior

Objective:

Defeat a Korblox enemy controlling a classic location.

Reward:

- Boss badge

## Restore The Timeline

Objective:

Complete multiple challenges to repair damage caused by the Korblox.

Reward:

- Builder Tokens
- Special badge

Related:

[Story Timeline](../Story%20&%20Lore/Story%20Timeline)

## Find The Hidden Secret

Objective:

Discover a secret location hidden in a participating game.

Reward:

- Builder Tokens
- Secret badge

---

# Quest Chains

Quest chains connect multiple objectives together.

Example:

Find Lost Tix
→ Help Classic Builder
→ Defeat Korblox Enemy
→ Restore Timeline

Final reward:

- Builder Tokens
- Completion badge
