Badges show player achievements during the event.

Badges should reward exploration, skill, and completing important challenges.

---

## Main Badges

## Classic Explorer

Type:
Easy Quest Badge

Requirement:
Complete your first event quest.

Reward:
- Tixs
- Event progress

---

## Classic Veteran

Type:
Hard Quest Badge

Requirement:
Complete difficult event challenges.

Reward:
- Builder Tokens
- Exclusive rewards

---

## Boss Badges

Boss badges are earned by defeating important enemies.

Examples:
- Korblox Warrior Defeated
- Korblox General Defeated
- Korblox Leader Defeated

---

## Completion Badge

A final badge for players who complete the entire event.

Requirement:
- Complete all major quests
- Collect required rewards
- Defeat the final boss

Reward:
- Exclusive event reward
