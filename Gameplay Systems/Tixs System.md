Tixs are the main collectible currency of the event.

They represent the classic Roblox era and are used to unlock classic clothing rewards.

---

## How Players Earn Tixs

Players can earn Tixs by:

- Completing easy quests
- Exploring event games
- Finding hidden collectibles
- Completing daily challenges
- Participating in classic activities

---

## Tixs Purpose

Tixs are used for:

- Classic clothing
- Event-themed outfits
- Other affordable rewards

See:
[Classic Clothing Rewards](../Rewards/Classic%20Clothing%20Rewards.md)

---

## Design Goal

Tixs should be easy for most players to earn.

They represent the feeling of collecting classic Roblox items and should allow everyone to participate.

---

## Tixs Economy

Tixs should not be extremely rare.

Players should feel rewarded for playing, exploring, and completing quests.
