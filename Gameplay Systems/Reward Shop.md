The reward shop allows players to spend event currencies on exclusive rewards.

---

## Purpose

The shop gives players a way to use the currencies they earn during the event.

Players can exchange:

- [Tixs](Tixs%20System) for classic rewards
- [Builder Tokens](Builder%20Tokens%20System) for exclusive rewards

---

## Tix Shop

Tixs are used for rewards available to all players.

Possible rewards:

- Classic shirts
- Classic pants
- Basic accessories
- Event collectibles

Related:

[Tix Shop](../Rewards/Tix%20Shop)

---

## Builder Token Shop

Builder Tokens are used for rare rewards.

Possible rewards:

- Full UGC outfits
- Limited accessories
- Special collectibles

Related:

[Builder Token Shop](../Rewards/Builder%20Token%20Shop)

---

## Shop Design

The shop should feel like a classic Roblox catalog.

Features:

- Simple interface
- Item previews
- Clear prices
- Reward descriptions

---

## Purchase System

The system should:

- Check player currency
- Remove currency after purchase
- Give the reward
- Save ownership
