Builder Tokens are the premium event currency.

They represent the creativity, building, and community spirit of classic Roblox.

---

## How Players Earn Builder Tokens

Builder Tokens are earned through harder challenges.

Examples:

- Completing hard quests
- Defeating bosses
- Completing difficult game challenges
- Finding secret rewards

---

## Builder Token Purpose

Builder Tokens unlock exclusive classic-themed UGC rewards.

See:
[Builder Token UGC Rewards](../Rewards/Builder%20Token%20UGC%20Rewards.md)

---

## Design Goal

Builder Tokens should feel rare and valuable.

Players should feel a sense of accomplishment when earning them.

---

## Difference Between Tixs and Builder Tokens

Tixs:
- Easier to earn
- For everyone
- Used for classic clothing

Builder Tokens:
- Harder to earn
- More exclusive
- Used for UGC rewards

---

## Symbolism

Builder Tokens represent the builders, developers, and creators who shaped Roblox.
