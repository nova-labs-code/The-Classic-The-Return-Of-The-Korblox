Player progression tracks a player's journey through the event.

---

## Progression Path

Players begin by exploring the event hub.

Progression:

Explore Event Hub
→ Complete Easy Quests
→ Earn Tixs
→ Unlock Classic Clothing
→ Complete Hard Quests
→ Earn Builder Tokens
→ Unlock UGC Rewards
→ Complete The Event

---

## Player Data

The event should track:

- Tixs collected
- Builder Tokens collected
- Completed quests
- Earned badges
- Purchased rewards
- Event completion

---

## Progression Goals

Players should always have a goal to work toward.

Goals include:

- Completing quests
- Collecting rewards
- Defeating bosses
- Finding secrets

---

## Completion

Players who finish the event earn:

- Completion badge
- Final reward
- Recognition as a Classic Defender
