The style guide defines the visual direction for The Classic: The Return of Korblox.

The goal is to combine classic Roblox nostalgia with modern quality.

---

## Overall Style

The event should feel like:

- Classic Roblox
- Community-created worlds
- Exploration
- Adventure
- Nostalgia

The design should remind players of older Roblox while still feeling polished.

---

## Environment Style

Recommended:

- Simple shapes
- Blocky structures
- Bright colors
- Classic materials
- Clear layouts

Avoid:

- Overly realistic environments
- Dark modern designs
- Excessive visual effects

---

## Character Style

Characters should use:

- Classic proportions
- Simple clothing
- Recognizable designs
- Memorable silhouettes

Important themes:

- Builders
- Guests
- Explorers
- Warriors
- Classic players

---

## Korblox Style

The Korblox should feel different from the classic world.

Their design should include:

- Darker colors
- Futuristic elements
- Advanced armor
- A threatening appearance

The contrast should show that they do not belong in the classic timeline.

---

## UI Style

Event UI should include:

- Classic-inspired designs
- Simple menus
- Clear objectives
- Easy navigation

Recommended elements:

- Brick patterns
- Old Roblox-inspired icons
- Classic colors

---

## Reward Style

Rewards should be:

- Recognizable
- Collectible
- Worth showing off

Players should immediately know an item is from the event.
