The soundtrack creates the feeling of exploring different eras of Roblox history.

---

## Purpose

Music should help players feel:

- Nostalgia
- Adventure
- Mystery
- Danger

---

# Music Categories

## Event Hub

Purpose:

Create a welcoming classic Roblox atmosphere.

Style:

- Classic
- Adventurous
- Calm

---

## Classic Era Areas

Purpose:

Represent older Roblox worlds.

Style:

- Retro
- Simple
- Nostalgic

---

## Exploration Music

Purpose:

Play during quests and discoveries.

Style:

- Mysterious
- Curious
- Adventurous

---

## Boss Music

Purpose:

Create excitement during major battles.

Style:

- Intense
- Dramatic
- Powerful

Related:

[Bosses](../Story%20&%20Lore/Bosses)

## Korblox Theme

Purpose:

Represent the invasion and timeline damage.

Style:

- Dark
- Futuristic
- Threatening

Related:

[Korblox Lore](../Story%20&%20Lore/Korblox%20Lore)

---

## Credits Music

Purpose:

Celebrate players completing the event.

Style:

- Emotional
- Memorable
- Classic

---

## Music Requirements

Music should:

- Fit the classic Roblox feeling
- Be properly licensed or approved
- Match the area it is used in
- Avoid distracting players from gameplay
