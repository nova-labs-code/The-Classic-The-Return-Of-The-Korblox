UGC rewards are exclusive items created for The Classic: The Return of Korblox.

UGC should represent classic Roblox culture while feeling like valuable collectibles.

---

## Design Rules

UGC should have:

- Classic Roblox inspiration
- Simple recognizable designs
- Strong identity
- Nostalgic feeling

Avoid:

- Overly modern designs
- Excessive details
- Designs that do not match the classic theme

---

## Accessory Ideas

## Tix Crown

A crown inspired by the classic Tix currency.

Theme:
The return of a lost piece of Roblox history.

---

## Time Capsule Backpack

A backpack containing items from different Roblox eras.

Theme:
Preserving Roblox memories.

---

## Classic Toolbox

A creator-themed accessory.

Theme:
The builders who created Roblox worlds.

---

## Korblox Relic

A mysterious item connected to the Korblox invasion.

Theme:
A piece of the future brought back through time.

---

## Legacy Trophy

A reward showing that a player completed difficult challenges.

Theme:
Recognition for protecting Roblox history.

---
