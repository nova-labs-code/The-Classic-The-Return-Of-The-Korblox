Maps should capture the feeling of classic Roblox while supporting modern gameplay.

---

## Classic Hub

The main event location.

Features:

- Classic Roblox buildings
- Quest area
- Reward shop
- Tixs collection area
- Builder Token showcase

---

## 2008 World

A world inspired by early Roblox.

Features:

- Simple terrain
- Classic building style
- Old-style environments
- Beginner quests

---

## Korblox Invasion Area

A classic world taken over by the Korblox.

Features:

- Corrupted classic locations
- Enemy encounters
- Boss battles

---

## Time Machine Area

The location where players travel between eras.

Features:

- Story progression
- Timeline information
- Event updates

---

## Secret Areas

Hidden locations for exploration.

Possible secrets:

- Lost Tix vault
- Hidden developer room
- Classic myths area
- Rare collectibles
