The event hub is the central location where players begin their journey.

---

## Purpose

The hub connects all parts of the event.

Players can:

- Learn the story
- Start quests
- View rewards
- Travel to participating games
- Track progress

---

## Main Areas

## Spawn Area

The starting location.

Includes:

- Event introduction
- Story explanation
- Quest guides

---

## Reward Area

Players can view:

- [Classic Clothing Rewards](../Rewards/Classic%20Clothing%20Rewards.md)
- [Builder Token UGC Rewards](../Rewards/Builder%20Token%20UGC%20Rewards.md)

---

## Quest Area

Players can see:

- Active quests
- Completed quests
- Progress

---

## Game Portal Area

Contains portals to:

- [Participating Games](../Games/Participating%20Games.md)

- Korblox invasion
- Timeline information
