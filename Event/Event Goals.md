The goals of The Classic: The Return of Korblox.

---

## Main Goal

Create a Roblox event that celebrates classic Roblox culture while giving players a new adventure.

---

## Player Goals

Players should be able to:

- Explore classic-inspired worlds
- Complete quests
- Collect [Tixs](../Gameplay%20Systems/Tixs%20System)
- Earn [Builder Tokens](../Gameplay%20Systems/Builder%20Tokens%20System)
- Unlock rewards
- Experience the story

---

## Community Goals

The event should:

- Bring developers together
- Showcase community experiences
- Encourage exploration
- Create memorable moments

---

## Long-Term Goal

Create an event that players remember as a celebration of Roblox history.
